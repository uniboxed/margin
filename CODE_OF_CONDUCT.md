# Code of conduct

Margin follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the
person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere
else the project is.

To report behaviour that breaks this, contact the maintainer privately through
[the repository's Security tab → Report a vulnerability](../../security/advisories/new) (it's private, and it's read
by the maintainers only) and say it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainers may
warn, remove content or ban anyone who breaks this code.
