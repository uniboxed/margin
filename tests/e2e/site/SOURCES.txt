Where the test site's pictures, sounds and fonts come from.

Nothing is downloaded, hotlinked or copied from another site. Every name,
story, product and person is made up.

Pictures (art.js)
  SVG drawn from code with a seeded random source: landscapes, a city, a
  coast, abstract work, products, avatars, a map. Served at
  /media/art/<kind>.svg on every site. Inline SVG icons: sites/common.js.

Icons, the tune (make-media.mjs)
  PNG and ICO icons are drawn into pixels by the script and encoded by it
  (zlib only). The podcast's tune (WAV) is synthesised by it. All made in
  memory when the server starts; none of it is committed.

Fonts
  System fonts (-apple-system, system-ui, ui-serif, ui-monospace,
  ui-rounded) everywhere but the portfolio.
  noir.test uses Archivo Black and Space Grotesk, SIL Open Font License 1.1,
  served straight from extension/themes/neo-brutalism/fonts/ (their
  SOURCES.txt and OFL files are there). Not copied.
